Your academic data stays in your browser.

Smart UMN is an independent student planning tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by the University of Minnesota. Official APAS and UMN registration systems remain authoritative.

What stays local

The extension reads an APAS audit only on official UMN uAchieve pages after you sign in there. It keeps normalized academic records and requirements in Chrome storage. The Web stores its planning copy and saved plans in this browser. Raw authenticated HTML, passwords, cookies, Duo codes and SAML data are not uploaded to our API.

Public evidence requests

Course identifiers, campus and term are sent to the public-evidence API to retrieve UMN Schedule Builder and GopherGrades data. Review-only policy searches send a bounded requirement phrase and public program metadata. These queries are not stored or logged by the application. The service maintains a cache of public evidence and aggregate operational counters. Cloudflare provides HTTPS and network delivery and may process connection metadata under its own privacy policy. We use no advertising or analytics scripts.

External community sites

RateMyProfessors and Reddit links open the original sites. Those sites receive normal navigation information when you follow a link. Historical grades, professor ratings and community discussion are context, not predictions or degree authority.

Delete your data

Use “Forget local academic data” in the connection panel, clear this site's browser data, and remove the extension if desired. Removing the extension alone does not clear the separate Web storage. Browser storage is not application-level encrypted; someone with access to your device or browser profile may access it.

Planning limits and contact

Unknown requirements remain subject to advisor review. Smart UMN cannot guarantee registration, graduation or a particular grade. For privacy questions, contact the project maintainer at QinyangTan on GitHub. Do not send private audits, student IDs or credentials in public issues.

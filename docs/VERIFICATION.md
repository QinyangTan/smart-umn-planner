## Requirement-route verification boundary

The parser now separates **strict completion routes**, **candidate routes**, and **policy/accounting constraints**. Exact selectable-course rules, proven count/credit semantics, exact Twin Cities Liberal Education categories, and deterministic labels such as `4xxx/5xxx-level <SUBJECT> coursework` may become strict. Explicit APAS pools containing unresolved caps/exceptions remain candidate-only. Parser 0.4.5 uses an explicit non-authorizing `policy` Rule IR. In addition to degree-credit, institutional-GPA, residency, final-residency, major-credit, upper-division-major-credit, and scoped-designator accounting prose, it recognizes qualified official-category requirements and degree-application/accounting scope. The saved real Computer Science audit has two raw strict course routes, one candidate Technical Electives route, seven non-authorizing aggregate containers, and nine actual policy/accounting constraints across nineteen open nodes; all nine policy constraints are now typed and zero remain unclassified. Official Liberal Education enrichment may add strict/candidate attribute routes only from current Schedule Builder metadata; qualified category wording such as a lab/field condition remains candidate-only until the qualifier itself is proven.

A primary APAS program can retain additional explicitly imported program audits such as a second major, minor, or certificate. Requirement IDs are namespaced by program; the primary audit is never silently replaced or all historical/what-if audits automatically merged.

## v0.9.7 prerequisite parser v2 + honest prerequisite coverage — 2026-09-28

Prerequisite parsing now receives the current course subject from Schedule Builder, allowing subject-omitted shorthand such as `AEM 8202` with `prereq: 8201` to become the explicit `AEM 8201` rule without guessing across subjects. The parser also handles top-level comma/semicolon AND lists, bracketed OR course groups, explicit minimum letter grades, trailing punctuation, historical `(previously COURSE)` annotations, and clearly non-binding recommendation-only clauses. Ambiguous recommendation scope, consent, standing, program/major membership, honors, placement tests, auditions, concurrent/corequisite conditions and similar policy conditions remain review-only. Mixed rules such as `course + instructor consent` are classified as partially structured and never become schedule-eligible until the entire rule evaluates to `yes`.

Reclassifying the same pre-v2 snapshot with this stricter metric gives a truthful old baseline of 100 deterministic / 192 partial / 2,642 review-only, so parser v2 adds **234 fully deterministic courses** and reduces fully review-only courses by **582** without promoting consent/standing/corequisite ambiguity.
